Privacy Policy

Short version: Sorta collects nothing.

Sorta runs entirely on your Mac. It has no network access, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting of its own.

To do its job, Sorta reads the names, sizes and dates of files in the folders you choose, and moves them according to your rules. Your rules, settings and the activity log (used for Undo) are stored only on your Mac, inside Sorta’s own app container. Removing the app removes them.

Sorta does not open or read the contents of your files.

If you email support, your email is used only to answer you.
